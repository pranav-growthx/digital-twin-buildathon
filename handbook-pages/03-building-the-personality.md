# Building the Personality

> 11:30–12:00 · core · done on Claude web.

---

## Starter file

Give **[IDEATION.md](starter-files/IDEATION.md)** to Claude / ChatGPT — it runs a quick interview about how you text and produces your personality file.

---

Define **how your agent talks** — its tone, humor, message style, and boundaries. No code in this phase. **Takes 15–20 minutes.**

> Do this on **Claude web (claude.ai) or ChatGPT — not Claude Code.** It keeps your Claude Code usage fresh for the actual build later.

## Steps

1. Open **claude.ai** (or ChatGPT) in your browser.
2. Paste in the **`IDEATION.md`** rules from the starter files above.
3. Tell it: **"Run my digital-twin interview."** That one line kicks it off.
4. It walks you through **9 quick questions, one at a time** — message shape, punctuation style, emojis, tone, greetings, humor, topics, boundaries, and pronouns. Each is a tick-box: put an `x` in the brackets next to what fits you and send it back. Unsure on one? It picks a sensible default and moves on.
5. It writes you a **`PERSONA.md`** — your voice and style in one file. Save it into your `my-twin` folder.
6. It suggests a few **names** — pick one. You should walk out knowing your agent's name.

> The name matters — you'll introduce your agent by name when you demo it on stage.

## Quick sanity check

Before you move on — test it. Paste your `PERSONA.md` as a system prompt in Claude or ChatGPT, then text it something a friend would say. Does the reply sound like you? If it's way off, tweak the persona before you build. 30 seconds now saves rework later.

> This is **v0.1** of your personality — good enough to build with. You'll refine it once you see your twin in action and notice where it sounds off.

When you have a saved `PERSONA.md` and a name, you're ready to build.
